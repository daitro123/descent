# What makes a single hit feel weighty at the slower tempo?

Type: grilling
Mode: HITL
Status: open
Blocked by: 05

## Question

Define the anatomy of a hit that applies to both the Warrior and the enemies: wind-up, active and recovery windows; whether attacks can be cancelled; hit-stop; knockback and stagger; screen shake; hit flash; whether there's stamina or another limit on spamming attacks; how damage and health scale (few big hits or many small ones). The outcome is the list of parameters the Tuning panel exposes and their starting values.
